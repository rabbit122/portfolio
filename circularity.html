Circularity

01 — Overview

Project Overview

Circularity is a B2B SaaS platform built for waste management companies seeking to digitise and optimise their collection and recycling workflows. The platform surfaces real-time data across the full waste lifecycle — from collection scheduling to material recovery tracking — enabling operators to make faster, more informed decisions.

My role spanned the end-to-end design process: from initial discovery and stakeholder alignment through to high-fidelity prototyping and handoff. Working closely with product managers and engineers, I translated complex operational requirements into a coherent, usable interface.

02 — Challenge

The Problem Space

Waste management operations are inherently complex — they involve multiple stakeholders, time-sensitive logistics, and regulatory reporting requirements that vary by region. Existing tools were fragmented: operators relied on spreadsheets, legacy ERPs, and manual paper trails that made it nearly impossible to get a unified view of performance.

The core challenge was designing a system that could surface the right information at the right moment without overwhelming users who were often working in fast-paced, high-pressure environments. This meant ruthless prioritisation of what to show, and careful consideration of how information hierarchy could guide decisions without adding cognitive load.

03 — Process

Design Process

I began with a discovery sprint — conducting contextual interviews with field operators, depot managers, and sustainability leads to understand the distinct mental models at each level of the organisation. These sessions surfaced a recurring tension: field staff needed speed and clarity, while management needed depth and auditability.

From those insights I mapped out user journeys and information architecture before moving into low-fidelity wireframes. Iterative usability testing with representative users shaped each round of design, surfacing friction points that weren't visible in the initial flows. The final design system was built in Figma with reusable components to support a fast-moving engineering team.

04 — Result

Outcome

The platform launched to a pilot group of operators and received strong early feedback — particularly around the clarity of the dashboard and the reduction in steps required to log and report collections. Internal testing showed a measurable reduction in time-on-task for the most common workflows.

The design system established during this project became the foundation for future feature development, enabling the team to ship new capabilities with greater speed and consistency. The project reinforced the value of spending time upfront in discovery — the decisions made early in the process paid dividends throughout delivery.
