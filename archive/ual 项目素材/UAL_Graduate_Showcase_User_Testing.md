# UAL Graduate Showcase  
## Public Website User Testing (Summer 2021)

Author: Karen Loasby, Dogwood Digital  
Date: August 2021  

---

## 1. Context

The UAL Graduate Showcase launched in summer 2020.  
The public-facing website was tested in January 2021 and redesigned before relaunch in summer 2021.

This research explores:
- How users move through the redesigned website  
- Their impressions of the site  
- Problems encountered  
- Opportunities for improvement  

---

## 2. Approach

Remote moderated usability testing with 5 target users:

1. Architect (Switzerland)  
2. Visual & motion designer (UK)  
3. Service designer (UK)  
4. UX manager (UK)  
5. UX designer (UK)  

Devices used:
- 2 Macs  
- 2 Windows PCs  
- 1 Android phone  

---

## 3. Headline Findings

### What users liked
- Overall look & feel  
- Research & process sections  
- Text alongside images  
- Videos  
- Student information  
- Tags and labels  

### Key problems
- Unclear starting point  
- Difficulty following interests  
- Hard to choose projects  
- Scrolling not obvious  
- Confusing summaries  
- Poor back navigation  

---

## 4. Homepage

### User Testing
- More scrolling than before  
- Structure confusing  
- Users unsure what to do next  

### Analytics
- Increased pageviews and entrances  
- Higher bounce rate (mobile driven)  

---

## 5. Key UX Issues

### Navigation
- No clear pathway  
- No breadcrumbs  
- Back navigation frustrating  

### Content Discovery
- Weak filtering  
- Random ordering  
- Hard to follow interests  

### Interaction
- Components confusing  
- Scroll not obvious  
- Mobile experience weaker  

---

## 6. Page-Level Insights

### Student Work
- Eye-catching but confusing  
- Order unclear  

### View All Work
- Needs filters  
- Low engagement  

### Projects
- Exploration improved  
- First impression depends on content quality  

### Gallery
- Expected larger images  
- Limited content  

### Research & Process
- Most valued section  

### Events
- High interest but confusing  

### Search
- Very low usage  

---

## 7. Analytics Insights

- Mobile traffic increased  
- Social traffic increased  
- Higher bounce rates linked to mobile  

---

## 8. Recommendations

### High Priority

#### Mobile Homepage
- Encourage scrolling  
- Reduce focus on single content block  

#### Course & College Pages
- Remove “false floor”  
- Improve project visibility  

#### View All Work
Add filters:
- College  
- Course  
- Subject  
- Theme  
- Collection  

---

### Medium Priority

#### Homepage
- Simplify layout  
- Remove unused components  
- Curate featured work  

#### Project Pages
- Shorten summaries  
- Improve readability  

---

## 9. Key Takeaways

- Visual design improved, but IA is weak  
- Users struggle with navigation and discovery  
- Mobile UX is critical  
- Collections layout performs best
